Minesweeper Help

Reveal every square that doesn't hide a mine. Numbers show how many mines touch that square.

  • Click to reveal a square. Your first click is always safe.
  • Right-click, Ctrl-click or ⌘-click to place a flag.
  • Click a number with the right flags around it to reveal its neighbours.
  • Choose Beginner, Intermediate or Expert from Game.
  • 🙂 or R: new game · H: help
